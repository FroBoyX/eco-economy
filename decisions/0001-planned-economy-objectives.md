# Decision 0001 — Planned Economy Objectives

**Status:** Accepted project doctrine  
**Applies to:** Eco 14 economy, profession pricing, government policy, and recycling design

## Decision

This project will operate a **planned economy** with intentional reference prices across professions.

The design target is not maximum profit and not laissez-faire price discovery. The target is a stable, legible economy in which every profession has useful work, early producers can earn a shallow positive margin, and improved efficiency creates increasing profitability over time.

Government policy will be designed alongside the economy rather than after it.

The civic structure is expected to develop through:

**Town → Country → Federation**

At each level, policy should balance three co-equal goals:

1. minimize pollution and resource waste;
2. maximize meaningful participation by all professions;
3. maintain steady technological and economic progression.

Recycling is a first-class profession and must be integrated into this model. Waste begins as a disposal liability where appropriate and, as recycling progresses, can become feedstock that re-enters the wider production economy.

## Consequences

- Canonical prices should be documented and changed deliberately.
- Profession efficiency should usually improve margin without requiring price increases.
- Government shops and subsidies are legitimate stabilization tools but should not casually replace player professions.
- Environmental law must account for economic effects on professions.
- Progression policy must account for pollution and resource effects.
- Recycling incentives must be tested for both environmental benefit and economic participation.
- Laws must be designed against actual Eco 14 mechanics, not natural-language intent alone.
- Major price changes require review of downstream products.
- Important corrections should be recorded in the repository so old chat context does not remain a hidden dependency.
