# Ironwood Research Upgrades — Basic through Modern

These are the three generic Self Improvement upgrade modules: **Basic Upgrade**, **Advanced Upgrade**, and **Modern Upgrade**.

Source: supplied Eco 14 Core plus the current Ironwood research-paper prices.

## Pricing rule

- Craft at **Self Improvement 1**, the minimum unlock.
- Self Improvement 1 reduces ingredients and labor by **5%**.
- Research-paper inputs cross into Self Improvement at their current Library/reference values.
- Generic `Basic Research`, `Advanced Research`, and `Modern Research` inputs use the cheapest valid paper in that tier.
- Labor remains 1 credit / 1,000 calories.
- Trash output carries the current disposal burden.
- These are reusable capital/efficiency modules, so Town Buy uses the ordinary **30% upgrade/capital margin**.
- Town Sell uses the normal Exchange spread, capped at +10 credits for goods above 100 Town Buy.

## Basic Upgrade basis

Core recipe:

- 2 Dendrology Research Paper Advanced;
- 2 Gathering Research Paper Basic;
- 1 Agriculture Research Paper Basic;
- 5 of any `Basic Research`;
- 1,500 calories;
- 0.2 Trash.

At Self Improvement 1 the effective inputs are 95% of the scalable recipe quantities. Culinary Basic remains the cheapest generic Basic Research input.

**Projected Cost: 52.50.**

## Advanced Upgrade basis

Core recipe:

- 2 Engineering Research Paper Modern;
- 2 Culinary Research Paper Advanced;
- 1 Agriculture Research Paper Advanced;
- 5 of any `Advanced Research`;
- 3,000 calories;
- 0.2 Trash.

The Engineering research correction materially changes this upgrade. Engineering Research Paper Modern is now **88.95 Town Sell** after the Engineering Advanced capital-sacrifice policy was set to a 45.00 transfer value.

At Self Improvement 1:

- 1.90 × Engineering Modern at 88.95;
- 1.90 × Culinary Advanced at 24.83;
- 0.95 × Agriculture Advanced at 31.16;
- 4.75 × cheapest Advanced Research, Gathering Advanced at 6.32;
- 2,850 calories;
- Trash disposal burden.

**Projected Cost: 278.70.**

## Modern Upgrade basis

Core recipe:

- 2 Metallurgy Research Paper Modern;
- 2 Agriculture Research Paper Modern;
- 1 Culinary Research Paper Modern;
- 5 of any `Modern Research`;
- 4,500 calories;
- 0.2 Trash.

Geology Modern remains the cheapest generic Modern Research input, so the Engineering correction does not change this recipe.

**Projected Cost: 526.06.**

## Market treatment

These are normal limited Exchange capital goods, not unlimited Library research-paper procurement items.
