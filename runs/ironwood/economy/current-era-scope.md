# Ironwood Current-Era Scope — Steam Entry

This run is currently **entering the Steam era**. Current Exchange prices are therefore set only by production routes that are legitimately available in the present economy.

## Current specialty boundary

For the current output pass, Ironwood treats **Tier 1–3 specialties as the active economy**:

- Farmer: Gathering, Farming, Milling, Fertilizers;
- Hunter: Hunting, Butchery;
- Chef: Campfire Cooking, Baking, Cooking;
- Carpenter: Logging, Carpentry, Shipwright, Paper Milling;
- Tailor: Tailoring;
- Mason: Mining, Masonry, Glassworking, Pottery;
- Smith: Smelting, Blacksmith;
- Engineer: Basic Engineering, Mechanics;
- Scientist: Painting, Recycling;
- Survivalist / Self Improvement: policy treatment rather than ordinary commodity imports.

## Future-transition specialties

The following are **not current price setters** for this run even when their source recipes have already been inspected for planning:

- Advanced Baking;
- Advanced Cooking;
- Cutting Edge Cooking;
- Advanced Masonry;
- Advanced Smelting / economy-wide Steel transition;
- Electronics;
- Oil Drilling / petroleum;
- Composites;
- Industry.

A future route does not retroactively lower a current price.

## Current-price rule

For this run:

1. use the least-cost **normal route actually available now**;
2. price its inputs at the current public cash cost when testing commercial viability;
3. preserve the depth-sensitive producer margin;
4. let individual skill, modules, talents and unusually advanced private infrastructure become producer profit;
5. do not reprice structurally until a later technology becomes broadly normal in Ironwood.

The eventual multi-era transition model will be maintained separately from the live run ledger.

## Important examples

- current Lubricant is animal-fat Grease, not petroleum Lubricant;
- current Sugar is the Huckleberry-era route until Beet Sugar is broadly normal;
- current Linen/Wool production uses current Loom-era routes, not later Automatic Loom efficiency;
- current metal prices use the present Bloomery/steam-entry chain, not future industrial recovery or petroleum-era processes;
- current vehicle prices use the Steam Mechanics chain and remain valid until a later economy-wide transition is deliberately declared.

## Recycling exception

Recycling is Tier 3 and is within the current technology boundary, but garbage prices remain **stream-specific and activation-sensitive**. A recovery recipe being technically available does not automatically make every garbage stream positive. The existing disposal schedule remains authoritative until a specific recovery stream is economically opened.
