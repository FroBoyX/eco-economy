# Reinforced Concrete

Normal Reinforced Concrete is now treated as a late-game structural material rather than remaining blocked behind Fiberglass.

The current recipe basis is:

- 1 Cement
- 2 Rebar
- 2 Sand
- 5 Crushed Rock
- 25 calories
- -> 5 Reinforced Concrete

At Masonry 4, using current Ironwood inputs, the recipe floor is approximately **0.64 per block**.

A simple ordinary-margin treatment would put the market below 0.80 per block, but that undervalues a Tier-4 structural material with a Steel/Rebar dependency and major late-game building demand.

Ironwood therefore establishes a structural-policy anchor:

**Reinforced Concrete: 1.00 Town Buy / 1.05 Town Sell.**

This is a deliberate bulk-construction policy value in the same spirit as Brick, Glass and Lumber rather than a pure recipe-floor price.

Fiber Reinforced Concrete remains a separate later material route and should not be confused with normal Reinforced Concrete.
