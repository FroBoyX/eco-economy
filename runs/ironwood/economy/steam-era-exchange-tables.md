# Ironwood Exchange — Steam-Era Shop Tables

This is the shop-facing publication for the **current Steam-entry economy**.

Detailed recipe arithmetic and Projected Cost remain in the profession ledgers under `runs/ironwood/professions/`. This publication deliberately carries the values needed to operate Exchange shops: **Town Buy** and **Town Sell**.

## Current baseline rules

- **Iron Bar is permanently anchored at 3.00 Town Buy / 3.15 Town Sell.** This is the current baseline economy, not a temporary shortage surcharge.
- Iron Ore, Crushed Iron Ore and Iron Concentrate are **not** raised with the bar anchor. The additional value is intentionally concentrated in Smelting conversion.
- Copper and Gold anchors are unchanged.
- Cross-specialty inputs use current Town Sell values when a profession row is recalculated.
- Existing nominal prices may remain unchanged where the higher input burden still fits the accepted commercial margin or where current Core evidence does not support a reliable structural rebuild.
- Bulk-policy Brick, Glass and Lumber retain their explicit construction-market prices.
- Research remains capped Library procurement rather than unlimited Exchange demand.
- Positive Recycling feedstocks are capped/managed procurement, not unlimited garbage demand.
- Future Oil, Electronics, Industry, advanced food, Composites, Advanced Masonry and other later efficiencies do not back-propagate into this Steam-entry baseline.

## Notes

- These are permanent **current Steam-entry baseline** prices. Future technology transitions may create a later baseline; the 3-Flake Iron Bar is not treated as a temporary market patch.
- The Mining side of the iron chain remains unchanged: Iron Ore **0.20 / 0.22**, Crushed Iron Ore **1.45 / 1.52**, Iron Concentrate **7.64 / 8.02**.
- The higher Iron Bar value is intentionally captured at Smelting and then propagated through verified iron-sensitive downstream recipes.
- Nominal prices are allowed to remain stable when existing margins absorb a small input increase; Ironwood does not create penny-level churn merely because Nails or a minor metal input changed.
- Deep Mechanics capital is held at the existing current nominal baseline when presently available recipe evidence cannot reproduce the accepted current calculation. That is a stable baseline decision, not a temporary exception.
- Detailed Projected Cost and recipe arithmetic live in the profession files and should be updated there first; this publication is the shop-facing Buy/Sell view.
