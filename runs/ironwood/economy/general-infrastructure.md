# Ironwood General Market Infrastructure

Some Core objects are not owned by a specialty but still need an Ironwood Exchange value because they are part of the economic loop.

## Store — skillless construction

Current Eco 14 Core Store recipe:

- 10 `Wood`
- 10 `Rock`
- 100 calories
- → 1 Store

The recipe uses generic `Skill` rather than a specialty, so there is no specialty resource multiplier to apply.

Using Exchange-sourced raw inputs:

- 10 Wood × 0.45 = 4.50
- 10 Rock × 0.11 = 1.10
- 100 calories = 0.10
- cash cost = **5.70**

A Store is capital infrastructure, but Ironwood also wants player-run input/import stores to be easy to establish.

**Store: 7.41 Town Buy / 7.78 Town Sell.**

This is inexpensive relative to the working capital required to stock a real material shop. The physical Store object should not be the barrier to market participation.
