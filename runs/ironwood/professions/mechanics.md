# Ironwood Mechanics — Current Steam-Era Industrial Table

Rebuilt directly from the supplied Eco 14 Core after canonicalizing commercially viable Metals, Blacksmithing, Basic Engineering, Lubricant, Pipes, Leather, Lumber, Glass and Shipwright components.

Mechanics is where Ironwood's depth-sensitive margin policy becomes most visible. Simple components receive shallow margins; deep machinery receives 25–40%; completed Steam vehicles receive the approved 50% terminal-good windfall.

Private sale remains unrestricted and may command more when vehicles are scarce.
