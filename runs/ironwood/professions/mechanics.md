### Cement Kiln — Mechanics 4, Assembly Line

Core:

- 8 Gearboxes
- 4 Pistons
- 16 Iron Plates
- 600 calories

At Mechanics 4:

- 5.2 Gearboxes × 15.72 = 81.744
- 2.6 Pistons × 10.36 = 26.936
- 10.4 Iron Plates × 2.57 = 26.728
- 390 calories = 0.390
- cash cost = **135.798**

Because it is a late Steam capital machine that opens Cement production, it receives the ~40% late-capital treatment:

**Cement Kiln: 190.12 Town Buy / 199.63 Town Sell.**

Cement itself is current Masonry 4 and supports current Asphalt Concrete. Reinforced Concrete remains future because its present Core route needs Fiberglass.

## Currently craftable but not normal current Exchange targets

Ironwood should not place unlimited public Buy orders for dormant future-use stock merely because a recipe is technically craftable:

- Combustion Engine / Generator — await liquid-fuel infrastructure;
- future-electric machines requiring Electric Machinist Table/circuits/Steel infrastructure;
- sorters requiring Reinforced Concrete;
- research books/papers — separate research policy.
