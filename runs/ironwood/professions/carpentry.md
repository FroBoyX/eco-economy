# Ironwood Carpentry — Current-Era Output Table

Rebuilt from the supplied Eco 14 Core after canonicalizing commercially viable Logging, Metals, Textiles, Pipes, Glass and Mechanics components.

Costs below use the actual minimum Carpentry level and **Town Sell prices for purchased inputs**. Margin then follows Ironwood's depth ladder: ordinary furniture ~15%, utility/storage ~20%, multi-step components ~18–25%, capital/workstations ~30%. Lumber remains on the Tier-3 construction policy.

## Lumber — Carpentry 1

Core:

- 10 `WoodBoard`
- 2 Nails
- 0.5 Flaxseed Oil
- 60 calories
- → 2 Lumber

At Carpentry 1:

- 8 Boards × 0.28 = 2.240
- 1.6 Nails × 0.16 = 0.256
- 0.4 Flaxseed Oil × 0.69 = 0.276
- 48 calories = 0.048
- cash cost/craft = 2.820
- cash cost/Lumber = **1.410**

The accepted Tier-3 construction value is therefore adjusted slightly to clear the commercial floor:

**Lumber: 1.62 Town Buy / 1.70 Town Sell.**

## Large door vs stockpile

The source-confirmed reason remains unchanged:

- **Large Lumber Door:** 20 Lumber + 30 Boards are static and receive no Carpentry resource-efficiency reduction;
- **Large Lumber Stockpile:** Lumber/Board inputs are skill-modified at Carpentry 5.

The new commercially viable relationship is therefore about **51.24 retail for the door vs 29.79 for the stockpile**.

## Remaining unpriced Carpentry rows

Only genuinely unresolved dependencies remain:

- Bookshelf / display cases and related outputs requiring **Paper**;
- Kitchen and later outputs requiring **Steel Bar** or generic advanced Fabric combinations;
- Carpentry upgrades/research outputs requiring Dendrology/Research papers;
- modern research/decor rows requiring Modern research inputs.

The basic/current Carpentry economy is no longer blocked by Plant Fiber, Linen, Cotton, Pipes, Leather, Glass, Light Bulbs or basic Mechanics components.
