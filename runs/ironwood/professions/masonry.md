## Cement — Masonry 4, Cement Kiln

Core:

- 2.5 Quicklime
- 1 Clay
- 70 calories
- -> 2 Cement
- Cement Kiln consumes 50 W fuel

At Masonry 4:

- 1.625 Quicklime × 0.84 = 1.365
- 0.65 Clay × 0.11 = 0.072
- 45.5 calories = 0.046
- effective 1.04-minute Kiln burn ≈ 0.203
- total/craft ≈ 1.685
- cash cost/Cement ≈ **0.842**

With shallow processing margin:

**Cement: 0.94 Town Buy / 0.99 Town Sell.**

Cement is current Steam-era Masonry. Reinforced Concrete is not current because its present Core route requires Fiberglass.

## Current/future boundary

- Reinforced Concrete — Fiberglass/Plastic route;
- Fiber Reinforced Concrete — Fiberglass;
- Advanced Masonry specialty outputs;
- research papers/skill books — separate research policy.

This corrects the earlier assumption that Cement itself belonged to the future Advanced Masonry era.
