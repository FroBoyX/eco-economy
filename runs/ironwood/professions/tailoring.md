## Important current value: Farmer Shirt

The high **114.06 Town Buy** is source-driven: current Core uses 30 Wool Fabric at Tailoring 5 plus 1,200 calories. The current hunted-Wool chain is expensive. A future textile technology transition may collapse this price, but it remains correct for the current run.

## Current-run boundary

Remaining major Tailoring blockers are genuinely future materials:

- Nylon Fabric / Nylon Thread / Nylon;
- Epoxy / Synthetic Rubber / Plastic;
- Steel Springs, Steel Bars or Steel Plate;
- research-paper rows under the research economy.

Decorative tag-product variants should inherit their parent family price during final presentation rather than receiving fabricated extra labor.
