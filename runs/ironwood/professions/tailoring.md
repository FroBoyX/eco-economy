## Notable source-driven values

### Farmer Shirt

The very high **114.06 Town Buy** is not a typo. Current Core requires **30 Wool Fabric** at Tailoring 5 plus 1,200 calories. Early Wool Fabric is expensive because its yarn comes from hunted Shorn Wool. This is exactly the kind of price that may collapse later when advanced wool processing becomes economy-wide.

### Canvas

Both Cotton and Linen routes exist. The current Linen route is the least-cost legitimate route and therefore controls the shared Canvas market price:

**Canvas: 2.50 / 2.62.**

- Gathering/Engineering/Basic Research papers — research rows.

Several decorative bunting/rug/sign variants are Core `AddTagProduct` variants that inherit a parent recipe family rather than defining independent labor. They should be normalized with their parent output during the final presentation pass rather than assigned a fake separate labor cost.

This means the current-era Tailoring economy is usable now; the remaining major clothing gaps are primarily **Nylon/Steel/chemical-era**, not missing agricultural foundation prices.
