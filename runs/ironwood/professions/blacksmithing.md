## Current-run boundary

Do not use future rows to set current prices:

- Sharpening Steel, Steel Saw Blade, Steel tools — Steel transition;
- modern tools — Fiberglass/Synthetic Rubber/Steel;
- Steel Gear — Industry/Epoxy;
- Steel-dependent decor/workstations;
- research/skill books — research economy.

Standing Basalt Pot remains pending until its exact parent Basalt-pot material route is normalized with the Mining/Masonry secondary-stone pass.
