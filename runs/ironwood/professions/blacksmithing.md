Rebuilt from Eco 14 Core using current metal, leather, wood, glass and Tallow values.

Blacksmithing spans shallow components, durable tools, current housing/decor, workstations and one-off civic capital. Steel/modern material rows are future and do not set this run's prices.

The permanent current Ironwood Iron Bar baseline is **3.00 Town Buy / 3.15 Town Sell**. Verified current Blacksmith outputs that materially consume Iron Bars or Iron Plates are repriced from that baseline below. Copper, Gold and upstream Mining prices are unchanged.

## Current-run boundary

Do not use future rows to set current prices:

- Sharpening Steel, Steel Saw Blade, Steel tools — Steel transition;
- modern tools — Fiberglass/Synthetic Rubber/Steel;
- Steel Gear — Industry/Epoxy;
- Steel-dependent decor/workstations;
- research/skill books — research economy.

Standing Basalt Pot remains pending until its exact parent Basalt-pot material route is normalized with the Mining/Masonry secondary-stone pass.
