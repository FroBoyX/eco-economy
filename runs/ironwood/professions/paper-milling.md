# Ironwood Paper Milling — Current Foundation

Rebuilt from the supplied Eco 14 Core using the current Natural Fiber anchors.

Paper Milling is important because `Paper` and `Cellulose Fiber` unblock Carpentry, Tailoring, research and later printing. Current Core explicitly tags Kelp, Plant Fibers, Flax Fiber and several flowers as `NaturalFiber`, so the least-cost legitimate current route can be calculated without guessing Wood Pulp.

## Cellulose Fiber — Paper Milling 1

Core:

- 12 `NaturalFiber`
- 60 calories
- → 1 Cellulose Fiber

Kelp is a valid `NaturalFiber` and currently sells publicly for 0.04. At Paper Milling 1:

- 9.6 Natural Fiber × 0.04 = 0.384
- 48 calories = 0.048
- Exchange cash cost = **0.432**

With a shallow processing margin:

**Cellulose Fiber: 0.48 Town Buy / 0.50 Town Sell.**

This does not make Kelp itself expensive; the value appears in the specialist conversion.

## Paper — Paper Milling 1

Core:

- 2 Cellulose Fiber
- 20 calories
- → 6 Paper

At level 1:

- 1.6 Cellulose Fiber × 0.50 = 0.800
- 16 calories = 0.016
- cash cost/craft = 0.816
- cash cost/Paper = **0.136**

**Paper: 0.15 Town Buy / 0.16 Town Sell.**

## Paper Milling Upgrade — level 2

Core:

- 32 Paper, static
- 6,000 calories
- → 1 Paper Milling Upgrade

At level 2:

- 32 Paper × 0.16 = 5.120
- 4,500 calories = 4.500
- cash cost = **9.620**

**Paper Milling Upgrade: 12.51 Town Buy / 13.14 Town Sell.**

## Still blocked Paper Milling outputs

- Printing Supplies and posters need **Ink**;
- Waxed Paper needs **Paraffin**;
- Dendrology Research Paper Modern needs Waxed Paper, Ink and the research chain.

Those are later Painting/Oil Drilling/research dependencies, not missing Paper economics.
