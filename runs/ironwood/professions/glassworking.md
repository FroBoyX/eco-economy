# Ironwood Glassworking — Glass Foundation

Derived from Eco 14 Core using current Ironwood anchors and entry-skill efficiency.

Derived producer costs use Town Buy/producer values for intermediate inputs. The Exchange retail spread is applied after the producer value is established.

## Limestone policy

Limestone remains strategically distinct from the common 0.10 rock tier.

**Limestone: 0.15 Town Buy / 0.17 Town Sell.**

This premium is justified by exact downstream demand in Quicklime, Glass, Cement and later industry.

## Basic Glass — Glassworking 1, Glassworks

Core recipe:

- 4 Sand, skill-modified
- 1 Crushed Limestone, static
- 30 calories
- output 1 Glass
- base craft time 1.2 minutes

Glassworks consumes Burnable Fuel at 50 W.

At Glassworking 1:

- 3.2 Sand × 0.10 = 0.320
- 1 Crushed Limestone × 0.64 = 0.640
- 24 calories = 0.024
- effective craft time = 0.96 minutes
- fuel ≈ 0.168
- modeled entry floor ≈ **1.152**

Glass is a Tier-3 bulk building material and receives a modest Ironwood construction/logistics premium above the bare recipe floor.

**Glass: 1.45 Town Buy / 1.52 Town Sell.**

This keeps Glass in the middle of the Tier-3 structural band: above Brick because it requires strategic Limestone, dedicated Glassworking and substantial fuel, but below Lumber.

- 150 calories
