# Ironwood Painting — Current Steam-Era Table

Rebuilt from the supplied Eco 14 Core using only current Tier 1–3 production routes.

Painting is a Tier-3 specialty and is therefore inside the current Steam-entry economy. Current pigments use the ordinary Mill routes; later Industrial Mill efficiency does not set the current price.

## Current pigment inputs

### White Powder — Milling 2, Mill

Core current route:

- 1 Trillium Flower
- 24 calories
- -> 5 White Powder

At Milling 2 (0.75), with Trillium Flower at 0.06 public Sell:

- 0.75 flower = 0.045
- 18 calories = 0.018
- cash cost/output ≈ 0.013

With Ironwood's minimum practical spread:

**White Powder: 0.02 Town Buy / 0.03 Town Sell.**

The later Industrial Mill route is more efficient but is outside the current price-setting boundary.

### Colored Powder — Milling 2, Mill

Core:

- 4 `Petals`
- 24 calories
- -> 1 Colored Powder

Current gathered flowers satisfy the Petals tag and sell for about 0.06 each.

At Milling 2:

- 3 Petals × 0.06 = 0.180
- 18 calories = 0.018
- cash cost = 0.198

**Colored Powder: 0.22 / 0.23.**

### Base Paint

Core makes 10 Base Paint from 4 White Powder + 0.5 Iron Bar + 2 generic Oil + 40 calories. At Painting 1 the public-input cash cost is about 0.193 per paint.

**Base Paint: 0.22 / 0.23.**

### Oil Paint

Core makes 4 Oil Paint from 4 Colored Powder + 2 Flaxseed Oil + 40 calories.

At Painting 1 the cash cost is about 0.468 per paint.

**Oil Paint: 0.52 / 0.55.**

### Art Supplies

Core makes 8 Art Supplies from 4 Canvas + 2 Oil Paint + 4 `WoodBoard` + 40 calories.

At Painting 1 using current Canvas 2.62, Oil Paint 0.55 and Board 0.28 public prices, the cash cost is about 1.274 per Art Supplies.

**Art Supplies: 1.50 / 1.58.**

### Ink — Painting 2

Core:

- 2 Charcoal Powder
- 1 Glass
- 1 generic Oil
- 180 calories
- -> 2 Ink

At Painting 2:

- 1.5 Charcoal Powder × 0.23 = 0.345
- 0.75 Glass × 1.65 = 1.238
- 0.75 Oil × 0.46 = 0.345
- 135 calories = 0.135
- cash cost/craft ≈ 2.063
- cash cost/Ink ≈ 1.031

**Ink: 1.19 Town Buy / 1.25 Town Sell.**

This is the current canonical Ink route and unlocks current Paper Milling printing outputs without Oil Drilling.
