# Ironwood Advanced Smelting — Steel Foundation

Rebuilt from the supplied Eco 14 Core. The current Steel market can be established without waiting for Coal or Oil because Core provides a **Charcoal Steel** recipe at Advanced Smelting 1.

Advanced Smelting products are deeper industrial intermediates. Steel Bar receives a ~15% margin over public-input cash cost; secondary Steel forms receive shallow follow-on margins; finished storage/doors receive ordinary finished-goods margins.

## Ceramic Mold dependency

Pottery 1 Ceramic Mold:

- 1 Clay
- 1 Sand
- 1 `Silica`
- 200 calories
- → 8 Ceramic Molds

Crushed Sandstone/Granite can satisfy `Silica` at 0.06 public Sell. At Pottery 1, cash cost is about **0.048 per mold**.

**Ceramic Mold: 0.05 Town Buy / 0.06 Town Sell.**

## Steel Bar — Charcoal Steel, Advanced Smelting 1

Core:

- 1 Iron Concentrate, skill-modified
- 1 Ceramic Mold, skill-modified
- 4 Quicklime, static
- 8 Charcoal, static
- 60 calories
- → 4 Steel Bars
- Blast Furnace, 50 W, base craft time 1.5 minutes

At Advanced Smelting 1:

- 0.8 Iron Concentrate × 8.02 = 6.416
- 0.8 Ceramic Mold × 0.06 = 0.048
- 4 Quicklime × 0.84 = 3.360
- 8 Charcoal × 1.30 = 10.400
- 48 calories = 0.048
- 1.2 effective minutes of 50 W fuel = 3,600 J = 0.18 Charcoal × 1.30 = 0.234
- total cash cost/craft = **20.506**
- cash cost/Steel Bar = **5.127**

With a 15% deep-intermediate margin:

**Steel Bar: 5.90 Town Buy / 6.20 Town Sell.**

This is the current Steel-era anchor. The Crushed-Coal Steel recipe may later improve producer economics when Coal supply is canonical, but a single alternate route does not force an immediate structural cut.
