# Ironwood Shipwright — Early Material Foundation

Current foundation rebuilt from the supplied Eco 14 Core because Basic Engineering depends on early Shipwright materials.

The full Shipwright profession includes boats, shipyards and late vessel components; those terminal goods will receive much larger late/terminal capital margins when audited. This file locks only the early cross-profession inputs needed now.

## Inputs

- Plant Fibers: **0.05 / 0.06**
- Wood: **0.40 / 0.45**
- Linen Yarn: **0.15 / 0.16**
- labor: **0.001 credit/calorie**

Shipwright 1 uses the 0.80 multiplier.

## Wooden Hull Planks — Shipwright 1

Core, Small Shipyard:

- 4 Plant Fibers
- 2 Wood
- 60 calories
- → 1 Wooden Hull Planks

At Shipwright 1:

- 3.2 Plant Fibers × 0.06 = 0.192
- 1.6 Wood × 0.45 = 0.720
- 48 calories = 0.048
- Exchange cash cost = **0.960**

As a multi-input construction/ship component:

**Wooden Hull Planks: 1.10 Town Buy / 1.16 Town Sell.**

## Hemp Mooring Rope — Shipwright 1

Despite the legacy name, current Core makes this from Linen Yarn:

- 6 Linen Yarn
- 180 calories
- → 1 Hemp Mooring Rope

At Shipwright 1:

- 4.8 Linen Yarn × 0.16 = 0.768
- 144 calories = 0.144
- cash cost = **0.912**

**Hemp Mooring Rope: 1.05 Town Buy / 1.10 Town Sell.**

## Deferred Shipwright scope

- canoes and boats;
- Wooden Barge / Transport Ship;
- Small/Medium Shipyard;
- fishing trawler components;
- Iron Hull Sheets and later vessel materials;
- terminal vessels.

Completed vessels should be reviewed under Ironwood's **late/terminal capital windfall** rule rather than ordinary component margins.
