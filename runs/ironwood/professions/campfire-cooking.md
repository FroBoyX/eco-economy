# Ironwood Campfire Cooking — Current Food Table

Rebuilt from Eco 14 Core using canonical Gathering, Hunting, Butchery and Milling inputs.

Prepared food uses the higher of:

- the locked energy anchor of 1 credit per 1,000 calories; or
- Exchange-sourced ingredient cost plus a modest cooking margin.

Freshness adjustments are applied separately.

## Tallow / Render Fat

Campfire Cooking 1: 10 Scrap Meat + 20 calories -> 5 Tallow. At level 1 this is 8 Scrap Meat + 16 calories, or about 0.163 cost per Tallow at current prices.

**Tallow: 0.20 Town Buy / 0.21 Town Sell.**

This controls early Fat pricing and supports Butchery Grease Lubricant at 0.14 / 0.15.
