# Ironwood Electronics — Current Early Electrical Foundation

Rebuilt from the supplied Eco 14 Core after canonicalizing Steel, Copper/Gold products and Glassworking.

Electronics is only partially open in the current material era. Core provides a complete early chain through Fuse -> Gold Flakes -> Basic Circuit -> Electric Motor without requiring Plastic or Oil Drilling. Advanced circuits and most modern machines remain blocked by Fiberglass/Epoxy/Plastic/chemistry/Industry materials.

## Fuse — Electronics 1

Two Core routes produce the shared Fuse item. The **Ceramic Fuse** route is the least-cost current route:

- 1 Clay
- 2 Copper Wiring
- 80 calories
- -> 4 Fuse

At Electronics 1:

- 0.8 Clay × 0.11 = 0.088
- 1.6 Copper Wiring × 1.70 = 2.720
- 64 calories = 0.064
- cash cost/Fuse = **0.718**

**Fuse: 0.80 Town Buy / 0.84 Town Sell.**

The Glass-based Fuse route is more expensive and serves as an alternate-input route rather than setting the market price.

## Current blockers

- Advanced Circuit — Insulated Copper Wiring + Substrate;
- Substrate — Fiberglass + Epoxy;
- modern lighting — Plastic and often Steel Plate/Composite Lumber;
- Servos — Fiberglass;
- Jaw Crusher / modern machinery — Synthetic Rubber, Steel Gear, Electric Motor;
- Solar/Wind generators — Servos, circuits and later Industry inputs;
- Handheld Camera/Remote Detonator — Plastic/Fiberglass/advanced circuits;
- Oil Drilling skill book — research economy.

The next material unlock is therefore **Oil Drilling / chemical materials and Industry**, not more basic electrical discovery.
